## Sonic the Hedgehog Slot Machine
A fun, Sonic-themed slot machine game built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Spin the 3 reels, manage your balance, and test your luck to hit the jackpot.

## Features
Starting Balance: Begin with $1,000 in your wallet to start playing immediately.

Two Betting Options: Choose between Min Bet ($5 per spin) or Max Bet ($20 per spin).

Classic Sonic Symbols: Uses image assets for 5 iconic characters and items: Rings, Dr. Eggman, Tails, Sonic, and Knuckles.

Jackpot Multiplier: Match all 3 symbols on a single spin to win 7x your bet ("Bang money team")!

Low Balance Warning: Displays a friendly notification when your balance drops too low to place a bet.

## How to Play
Place Your Bet: Click Min Bet ($5) or Max Bet ($20) to spin the reels.

Watch the Reels: The 3 slot images will change randomly to display Sonic, Tails, Knuckles, Eggman, or Rings.

Win or Lose:

3 Matching Symbols: You win a 7x multiplier payout added directly to your balance!

No Match: Your bet amount is lost 

Out of Funds: If your balance drops below your chosen bet, you will need to reset or reevaluate your budget before playing again.

## Code Overview
balance: Tracks current funds (starts at $1,000).

symbols: Array storing the file paths to all symbol images.

slotMachine(bet): Main JavaScript function that handles deducting bets, randomly selecting symbol images, checking win conditions, and updating the display.
